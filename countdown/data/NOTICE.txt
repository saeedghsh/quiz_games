Modifications: extracted the word column; normalized Arabic keyboard variants,
presentation forms and optional vowel marks; removed non-letter entries and
duplicates; sorted the remaining 85,705 surface forms. Internal Persian
half-spaces are retained. Hunspell affix rules are not expanded. Proper names
and uncommon words present in the source are included.

Reproduce from the pinned checkout:
python scripts/import_farsi_dictionary.py /path/to/lilak/src/data/lexicon countdown/data/fa_words.txt

The importer verifies the source SHA-256 before processing. No dictionary
downloads or external Python packages are needed when playing the game.
